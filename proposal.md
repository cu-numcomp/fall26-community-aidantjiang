### Notes/concerns/risks

* Contributor counts come from distinct commit emails, so they overcount people who use more than one address. Comment/email rates per week are rough estimates, not measured.

* SciPy is a large project with several sub-communities (optimize, linalg, special, stats, ...), so activity and review speed vary by module.
